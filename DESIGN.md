# Pages resources and compatibility

The personal site is maintained at `/personal-website/`. This repository remains a compatibility/resource host. Existing application association, authentication, advertising, privacy and legacy test resources retain their exact content and paths. The root test page is not presented as the personal website.

The two `/portfolio/` entry points keep immediate meta refresh, language-specific canonical URLs and noindex. The manual fallback adopts the shared paper #FAF8F3, ink #202421, copper #9B432F and muted #5E625C palette. Contrast is 14.81:1 for body, 5.86:1 for secondary text and 6.49:1 for white/copper action. Outfit is local with its complete SIL OFL notice. The heading scales from 36–60 px, the column is 720 px maximum with 24 px mobile gutters, and actions have a minimum height of 48 px and a 12 px radius. Links remain underlined and keyboard focus has a visible outline.

No countdown, script, tracking, spinner or decorative motion delays navigation. CSP permits only own-origin styles/fonts and keeps scripts/forms disabled. If refresh is blocked, text and manual links remain usable without JavaScript. QA uses a copy with only meta refresh removed to inspect this normally transient state; it is not claimed as the publicly served page. Existing compatibility assets, app resources and licenses remain unchanged.
